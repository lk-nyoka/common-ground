**Title**

Common Ground: an AI agent that plans one night two different tastes will both enjoy (Qloo Agentic Hackathon)

**Description**

My gogo loves Miriam Makeba. I love Tyla. Finding something we'd both enjoy? Not easy. So I built Common Ground.

Common Ground is an AI agent for two people with different taste. You each add a film, an artist and a series you love. It uses Qloo's taste graph to find where your tastes meet and plans one night: something to watch that fits your time, music to put on, and a swap each way from the other person's world.

Every pick shows how it ranks for each of you on your own. If a pick only suits one person, the agent swaps it out.

Also in the app:
• Mzansi match: South African artists when Qloo finds a South African thread in your shared taste
• Comedy: stand-up comedians ranked for both of you, with links to upcoming shows
• Apart? Send a link. The plan opens on both phones, with a chat beside it
• Swipe together, match, and press play at the same moment
• Date spots within 30 km, ordered for both tastes
• Story cards to share your night
• It learns from who liked what

Try it (free, no sign-up): https://common-ground-pho7.onrender.com
Code (MIT licence): https://github.com/lk-nyoka/common-ground

Built for the Qloo Agentic Hackathon with Next.js, TypeScript, the Qloo API and Google Gemini.
