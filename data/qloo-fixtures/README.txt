Real Qloo API responses are saved here when QLOO_CAPTURE=true and a live call succeeds.
Only files in this folder may be labelled "Qloo (saved)" in the app. Do not hand-edit them.

These files are git-ignored on purpose: Qloo allows private server-side caching, but their
terms say not to store Qloo response data in a public repository. Keep them on your machine
as a backup for local demos; the deployed app uses your live key.
